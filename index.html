Explain It Simply

Big ideas, in plain language. Start with the basics.

01

Technology · The basics

What is artificial intelligence?

AI is software that finds patterns in information and uses them to make a prediction or create something new. Think of it as a tool that learns examples—not a person that understands the world.

02

Business · The basics

Why do prices go up?

When many prices rise over time, your money buys a little less than before. This is called inflation. It can happen when making things costs more, or when lots of people want the same limited goods.

03

World · The basics

What does a climate target mean?

It is a goal for limiting pollution over a set period. Countries use targets to plan how to shift toward cleaner energy, while checking whether their actions are keeping pace with the goal.
